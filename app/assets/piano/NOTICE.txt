Salamander Grand Piano V3 sample subset
Author: Alexander Holm
Instrument: Yamaha C5 grand piano
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
Original source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano

The MP3 files are the velocity-15 conversions from
@audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity15, mirrored through jsDelivr:
https://www.npmjs.com/package/@audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity15

This project bundles 30 pitch-spaced samples from A0 to C8 for
offline browser playback. Notes between samples are retuned by playback rate;
this is not a full multi-velocity piano library.
